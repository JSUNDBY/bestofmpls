# Best of MPLS — Social Brand Kit

The look that makes every post unmistakably ours in three frames. Matches the
site exactly. Built for vertical video (Reels / TikTok / Shorts).

## Canvas
- **Size:** 1080 x 1920 (9:16 vertical)
- **Safe zone:** keep text inside the middle 80% vertically (TikTok/IG cover the
  top ~12% and bottom ~20% with UI). Title cards sit centered-high; lower-thirds
  sit just above the bottom UI band.

## Colors (use these hex values, no others)
| Role | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Background | `#0E0E10` | the dark canvas everything sits on |
| Text | `#F5F4F0` | headlines and body |
| Clay (accent) | `#C8200F` | eyebrows, the dot in the logo, chips, the one pop of color |
| Muted | `#B8B8B4` | secondary lines, captions under a headline |

Dark canvas only for social. It reads sharper on video than the light site and
makes the clay pop. One accent color, ever. Restraint is the brand.

## Type (Google Fonts, all free)
- **Headlines:** IBM Plex Sans, weight 700-800. Big, tight, confident.
- **Eyebrows / labels / chips:** Archivo, weight 600, UPPERCASE, letter-spacing ~0.14em.
- **Captions / body:** Source Sans 3, weight 400-600.
- Install once: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans · https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Archivo · https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Source+Sans+3

## The logo
`bestofmpls` in IBM Plex Sans bold, all lowercase, with a **clay period**: the
dot is always `#C8200F`. Never restyle it. Small, bottom corner, every video.

## The three templates (in this folder)
1. **title-card.svg** — the opening frame. Clay eyebrow (THIS WEEKEND, BEST BURGERS,
   etc.) over a big white headline. Hook them in the first half second.
2. **lower-third.svg** — tags a place mid-reel. Place name + a clay neighborhood
   chip, on a soft bottom scrim so it reads over any footage.
3. **end-card.svg** — the closer. Logo lockup + "Follow for daily picks" +
   bestofmpls.com. Always end here.

Edit the text in any vector tool (Figma, Illustrator, even a browser), export
to PNG with transparency, and drop the lower-third and end-card over your
footage in DaVinci. The title-card can be a full frame.

## Motion (keep it in the site's spirit)
- Text rises and fades in, never spins or bounces. 0.3-0.5s ease.
- One transition style throughout. Hard cuts between shots are fine and feel current.
- Let the food and the places be the motion. The graphics stay still and clean.

## Voice (captions + on-screen text)
- Warm, direct, local. Like a friend who actually knows the city.
- **No em dashes. No emojis.** (Hashtags are fine.)
- Name the specific thing: the dish, the deal, the move. Never "great vibes."
- Every caption ends pointing home: "Full list at the link" / "link in bio."

## Hashtag sets (mix base + the relevant lane)
- **Base:** `#minneapolis #saintpaul #twincities #onlyinmn #mnpls`
- **Food:** `#mplsfood #twincitieseats #mnfood #eatlocalmn`
- **Drink:** `#mplsbars #twincitiescocktails #mnbeer`
- **Events:** `#mplsevents #twincitieslife #thingstodomn`

## The funnel, every post
Video pulls a stranger in → caption sends them to the link → the link is the
newsletter signup + the site. Followers are nice; the newsletter is the asset.
