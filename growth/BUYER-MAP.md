# Best of MPLS — The Buyer Map

Who actually pays, for what, how much, and how to reach them. The model lives in
MODEL.md; this is the named, prioritized list of buyers behind it.

**The thing you're selling:** trusted attention from in-market locals who are
deciding where to go and spend, right now. Every buyer below is paying for access
to that audience, or for your skills in building their own version of it. Protect
the trust and the audience grows; sell the trust and there's nothing left.

Frame every name below as a *target*, not a commitment. The job is to qualify and
pitch, not assume.

---

## Tier 1 — fastest dollars (weeks)

### 1. Cannabis dispensaries  ← the standout
MN's recreational market is live, the operators have real marketing budgets, and
they are **largely locked out of Google and Meta ads** (federal illegality). A
trusted local guide is one of the few legal, brand-safe channels they have. That
makes willingness-to-pay high and competition for the channel low.
- **Buys:** featured listing, sponsored "where to go" placement, newsletter
  sponsorship, a dedicated dispensary guide page.
- **Price:** $99–299/mo listing; $250–750 newsletter sponsorship; $1.5–4k for a
  built microsite (services).
- **Warm lead:** Twin City Cannabis is already Josh's SEO client — the natural
  first call and the proof-of-concept case study. Then the other metro dispensaries.
- **Reach:** Josh's existing relationship first; then direct outreach, lead with
  "the ad channel you're allowed to use."

### 2. Bars, restaurants & music venues (featured listings)
The core recurring product. The businesses already in the guide who want to be
the one a reader picks tonight.
- **Buys:** featured/pinned listing, "tonight" priority, neighborhood-guide top spot.
- **Price:** $49–99/mo. Founding rate to the first 10.
- **Targets:** Icehouse (the plan's first call), Berlin, Crooners, the Dakota,
  breweries with budgets (Indeed, Surly, Sociable Cider, the reopening Dangerous
  Man — they *want* awareness), and the busy independents.
- **Reach:** start with venues you already cover well and can show traffic to; the
  ticket-referral UTM (already live on Icehouse links) is your proof you send buyers.

### 3. New openings (launch packages)
A new restaurant/bar has budget and urgency in its first 90 days and nowhere
better to announce itself to locals.
- **Buys:** a launch feature on /new/ + a newsletter mention + a social post.
- **Price:** $150–400 one-time, or roll into a 3-month listing.
- **Targets:** everything on the /new/ page and the openings the warm-lead feed surfaces.

## Tier 2 — recurring, needs the audience to grow (1–3 months)

### 4. Newsletter sponsors
Once the list is engaged (the editorial onesheet + the growth engine get it there).
- **Buys:** the "presented by" slot (already wired into the send), a dedicated section.
- **Price:** $100–500/send now; $500–1,600 as the list and open rate climb.
- **Targets:** breweries, dispensaries, local banks/credit unions, festivals,
  real-estate, anyone selling to "people who do things in the metro."

### 5. Event promoters, venues & festivals (event boosts)
Pay to push one specific show or festival to the top of the calendar/tonight.
- **Buys:** a featured event slot, a calendar pin, a newsletter blurb.
- **Price:** $50–150/event; festival packages $500+.
- **Targets:** touring promoters, the venues in Tier 1, festival orgs (Pride
  partners, Art-A-Whirl studios, Aquatennial).

### 6. Services clients (build their digital presence)
Your agency skills, with the site as the portfolio. The weak-web pitch list (the
next automation build) feeds this directly.
- **Buys:** a built site / SEO / "make me findable."
- **Price:** $1.5–4k per build, the fastest single chunk of cash.
- **Warm leads:** Twin City Cannabis (client), Watershed Spa (warm lead), then the
  guide businesses with broken or Facebook-only sites.

## Tier 3 — big and slow (6–12 months)

### 7. Tourism & civic
- **Buyers:** Meet Minneapolis (the CVB), Explore Minnesota, and the City's Dept of
  Arts & Cultural Affairs (Ben Johnson, public contact MPLSACA@minneapolismn.gov).
- **Buys:** content partnership, a sponsored "visitor" surface, a promotion contract.
- **Price:** project/contract scale ($2k–$16k+).
- **Reach:** the outreach drafts in OUTREACH-DRAFTS.md; relationship-led, not a cold sell.

### 8. Real estate & relocation
Realtors and property managers want to own the neighborhood guides the relocation
audience reads.
- **Buys:** neighborhood-guide sponsorship, a "new to the metro" placement.
- **Price:** $99–299/mo.

### 9. Affiliate / ticketing (passive)
Referral revenue from ticket links (UTM live on Icehouse). Small, but free money
once volume is there. Pursue an affiliate deal with a ticketing platform later.

## Guardrail (Carl)
Paid placement is fine and labeled. Paid *opinions* are not. The dispensary money
is the test case: take their ad, never their editorial. The day a reader can't
trust a pick is the day the audience, and every dollar resting on it, leaves.
