# @bestofmpls.mn — launch posts

Seven posts to open the account, in order. The first three need no shooting:
screenshots and the brand kit carry them. Post 1-2 days apart, then hand off
to the weekly pack (growth/social-pack.md) for cadence. Every claim below is
live on the site today; if a number drifts, trust the site.

Bio (set once): Field notes from the metro. An independent guide to
Minneapolis & St. Paul: 639 places, tonight's shows, zero paid rankings.
Bio link: https://bestofmpls.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=profile

### Rules that keep it on-brand
- No emojis, no exclamation points, no "amazing." The captions are the site's voice.
- Screenshots of the site ARE the visual identity: dark board, cool paper, clay accents.
- Never claim a number the site does not show that day.
- Post the board (concept 2) any quiet night; it is the signature and it is always true.
