# Best of MPLS — Launch & Money Plan

Built overnight from verified research. Honest, not hype. Everything here is
solo-doable in ~2-3 focused hours a day. Outreach drafts live in
`OUTREACH-DRAFTS.md`, the sales materials in `SALES-KIT.md`, funding detail in
`GRANTS.md`.

## The honest read

**What can actually happen in 2 weeks:**
- A first paid **services deal ($2,500-4,000)**. This is the only path that reliably puts real money in your pocket fast, and you already have the proof: bestofmpls is a live 640-page site you built solo. That IS the case study for "I'll build your local business's whole digital presence." No audience required.
- **One local-media writeup** that causes a real traffic spike, if you pitch a story (not a site). Racket or an Axios newsletter mention is the single most likely external spike.
- **One free shareable thing** (a map or a "new openings this month" tracker) seeded into r/minneapolis or a foodie's stories. A 640-page guide is noise; one sharp free thing spreads.
- **1-3 featured-listing clients** if you make the calls. The plumbing exists.

**What does NOT happen in 2 weeks (stop hoping):**
- A grant check. MSAB deadline is March 6, 2026, award Nov 2026. These are 6-12 month plays.
- A city or tourism contract. Real money exists there ($3k-5k creator deals are documented) but it's a relationship, not a two-week deal.
- Newsletter sponsorship money. That needs a list with a 40%+ open rate first.
- Grant money **for the for-profit guide itself.** Grant money is charitable. A for-profit guide is not. You'd fund a carved-out arts/community project, not the guide (see GRANTS.md).

**The strategic truth:** the site's near-term value isn't directory revenue. It's a working proof of capability that closes services deals, while the listings turn on recurring local cash. The newsletter is the multi-month engine (Naptown Scoop hit ~$200k/yr on ~18k subs; the money follows the list, not the page count).

## The 14-day blitz — two tracks daily: SPLASH (audience) and CASH (income)

**Days 1-2 — the shareable artifact + fill the plumbing**
- Build ONE free interactive thing: an "eat near [neighborhood/venue]" map OR a live "new Twin Cities openings this month" tracker. This is your viral unit. (I can build this; say which.)
- Fill the OpenTable affiliate ref (10 min, then ignore it).
- Finalize the founding-partner sell sheet + set up a payment link (Stripe link or similar).

**Days 3-4 — free distribution + warm up Reddit**
- Submit your strongest events to the free calendars: Meet Minneapolis, Visit Saint Paul, MSP Mag, Minnesota Monthly. Pure free distribution.
- Make a Reddit account and spend the week being genuinely helpful and link-free in r/minneapolis (answer "best patios / new spots / where to take guests"). Naked self-promo gets you banned.
- CASH: build a shortlist of 10 local businesses to pitch a services build (start with ones already in your guide that have weak websites).

**Days 5-6 — media pitches go out**
- Send the 3-4 tailored pitches in OUTREACH-DRAFTS.md: Racket, Axios, Minnesota Daily, Southwest Voices. Story-first, not site-first.
- CASH: first 3-5 services outreach emails/calls.

**Days 7-8 — seed the artifact + IG relationships**
- Post the artifact natively where it's genuinely useful (a real r/minneapolis thread; let people ask "where's this from?").
- Open reshare relationships with 2-3 micro TC food accounts via short personal DMs and a credit-and-tag post. Not mass DMs.

**Days 9-10 — sales push + Ben Johnson groundwork**
- CASH: 5-10 featured-listing calls using the sell sheet. Goal: 1-3 signed.
- Email the Department of Arts & Cultural Affairs (MPLSACA@minneapolismn.gov), the verified route to Ben Johnson, to open the relationship and ask about content needs + the Cultural Districts Arts Fund window.

**Days 11-12 — tourism content door + follow-ups**
- Pitch Explore Minnesota and Meet Minneapolis a creator/content sample reel (behind-the-camera is your edge). Aim at the documented $3k-5k creator-deal pattern.
- One polite nudge on the media pitches. Follow up services leads.

**Days 13-14 — convert + set the engine**
- Close whatever's warm. Get one invoice out.
- Make the newsletter signup obvious on the artifact and every page. It's the multi-month engine.
- Log what moved, double down on it.

## The money plan, ranked by time-to-first-dollar

1. **Services arm (web + AI for local business)** — days to 2 weeks, $2,500-4,000. The site closes the deal. Pitch 10 local businesses with weak sites. This dwarfs months of listing income.
2. **Featured listings** — 1-4 weeks, $49-99/mo each. Sell sheet + payment link + 5-10 calls. 5-10 listings = $300-800/mo recurring. No audience needed.
3. **City/tourism content contract (the Ben Johnson lead)** — 1-3 months, $3,000-5,000. Email the department; pitch Explore MN + Meet Minneapolis with a reel. Honest catch: the Cultural Districts Arts Fund funds district-specific activations, not a citywide guide (see GRANTS.md).
4. **Newsletter sponsorship** — 1-3 months, $50-150/send. Needs an engaged list first. Local newsletters command high CPMs because of geo-targeting, but only with real opens.
5. **Grant / fiscal-sponsor path** — 6-12 months, $2,000-10,000. Requires a carved-out nonprofit-purpose project (see GRANTS.md + the charter draft).
