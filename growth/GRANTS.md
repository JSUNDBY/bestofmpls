# Funding paths (verified June 2026)

**The hard truth first:** grant money is charitable, public-good money. Best of
MPLS as it stands (a for-profit LLC, brand new, commercial) is not eligible for
most of it. The realistic move is to carve out a genuinely public-good arts /
community project that is editorially distinct from the for-profit guide and ad
sales, get it fiscally sponsored, and apply with THAT. See
`nonprofit-charter-DRAFT.md`. This is a 6-12 month play, not fast money.

Coordinated by Ben Johnson's department. |

## The big-swing personal one
- **Bush Fellowship** — up to $150,000, funds YOU as a leader, not a project.
  Framed as your growth as a Twin Cities culture/journalism leader, this is a
  real (long, competitive) swing. Verify the current cycle at bushfoundation.org.

## The fiscal-sponsor mechanism (how a solo operator unlocks the rest)
- **Springboard for the Arts Incubator** (St. Paul, springboardforthearts.org/resources/incubator):
  a fiscal sponsor for individual artists. It lets a carved-out arts project
  receive grants and tax-deductible gifts under their 501(c)(3) umbrella.
  Rolling monthly review, free to apply, roughly $60-120/yr + a 7% fee on funds.
  Important: they CANNOT manage funds for for-profit purposes, so the sponsored
  project must be the genuine public-good one, not the commercial guide.

## The journalism money (mostly not a fit yet, logged for the record)
- **Press Forward (national)** — for-profits eligible in principle, but the open
  call excluded start-ups and required publishing since Sept 1, 2023. You fail
  the operating-history bar today. Watch pressforward.news/funding-opportunities.
- **Press Forward Minnesota** (housed at Minnesota Council on Foundations) — real
  and active, but targets established hyperlocal accountability newsrooms serving
  underserved communities. No open public application. Relationship only:
  pressforwardmn@mcf.org.
- **LION Publishers** — for-profit-friendly, a gateway to Google News Initiative
  $20k re-grants. Membership was paused into early 2026; requires you to have
  launched and meet bylined/transparency standards. Verify reopening at
  membership@lionpublishers.com.
- **Knight Foundation** — arts + journalism, long game, relationship-driven. Not
  an open application you can win cold.

## The honest sequencing
1. Now: the services and listings money (see SALES-KIT.md). That's the income.
2. Spring: apply to MSAB Creative Individuals (March 6 deadline) as an artist,
   if you can frame an honest media-arts practice project.
3. Parallel: open the Ben Johnson / Arts & Cultural Affairs relationship for a
   district project or content contract.
4. 6-12 mo: if it's real, stand up the carved-out public-good project + Springboard
   fiscal sponsorship, which unlocks MRAC and the rest.
