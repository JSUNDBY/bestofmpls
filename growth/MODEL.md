# Best of MPLS — The Money Model

Decision-ready. The Revenue Working Group's view of how this becomes a business.
Tactics and outreach drafts are in the rest of `growth/`; this is the model itself.

## The model in one sentence

Best of MPLS makes money three reinforcing ways, and the site is both the engine
and the proof: **(1) services** — building local businesses' digital presence,
for fast cash now; **(2) local ad revenue** — featured listings and newsletter
sponsorships, recurring, once the audience grows; **(3) a civic/grant-funded
public-good track**, parallel and mission-aligned. Each one makes the next easier.

## The three streams, with the math

| Stream | Unit | Realistic near-term | What it needs | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Services** (web + AI for local biz) | $1,500–4,000 / build | 1–2/mo = $3,000–8,000/mo | The site as proof + 10 pitches | Days |
| **Featured listings** | $49–99 / mo each | 10–20 = $500–2,000/mo recurring | A rate card + sales calls | 1–4 weeks |
| **Newsletter sponsorship** | $100+ CPM (local geo premium) | $400–1,600 / send later | An engaged list (1k+, 40%+ open) | 1–3 months |
| **Civic / grant track** | $2,000–16,700 / project | project-based | A carved-out public-good project + fiscal sponsor | 6–12 months |

Why this order: services need no audience (the proof already exists), listings
need a rate card and calls, newsletter money needs the audience the social engine
builds, and grants need a separate non-commercial project and time. You earn
while you build the thing that earns more.

## The path

- **Months 1–2 (first dollars):** Close 1–2 services builds (start with guide
  businesses that have weak sites). Sign 3–5 founding featured listings (start
  with Icehouse). Target: **$3,000–5,000/mo**, services-led.
- **Months 3–6 (build the audience):** Run the social + newsletter engine
  (`scripts/social-ideas.js` + the brand kit). Grow the list. Sell more listings,
  land the first newsletter sponsor. Open the Arts & Cultural Affairs relationship.
- **Months 6–12 (compounding + leverage):** Newsletter sponsorships scale with the
  list. Stand up the civic/grant track if it's real. Hire the first commission
  salesperson, funded by revenue, not your pocket. Target: **$8,000–12,000/mo**,
  mixed, toward a self-sustaining local media business.

## What we're betting on (and how to validate cheaply)

- **Will local businesses pay for a listing?** Test: 5 founding-rate calls. If 1–2
  say yes, the recurring model is real.
- **Will the audience grow?** Test: the GA4 `newsletter_signup` event we now track,
  by source. If social converts to signups at a steady clip, the newsletter
  revenue unlocks on schedule.
- **Will someone pay for the build?** Test: 10 services pitches. One yes funds a
  month. This is the lowest-risk bet and the fastest.

## The single first move

The Icehouse call and ten services pitches. Both use relationships and proof you
already have, and both can land real money inside two weeks. Everything else
compounds behind them.

## Honest guardrails (Carl)
The site's trust is the asset. Paid placement is fine and clearly labeled; paid
*opinions* are not. Keep the editorial picks unbought. A local guide that sells
its honesty has nothing left to sell.
