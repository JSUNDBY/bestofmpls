# Sales kit

Two offers. The services offer is your fastest real money. The featured-listing
sheet is the recurring local cash. Both use the site itself as the proof.

---

## A. Services offer — "I'll build your local digital presence"  [the fast money]

**The pitch in one line:** I built a 640-page guide to the entire Twin Cities by
myself. I can build your restaurant/bar/shop a site, menu, and digital presence
that actually works, and wire in the AI tools that save you hours.

**Why it lands:** you are not asking them to imagine it. bestofmpls.com is the
live proof. Send the link, then say "I'll do for your place what I did for the
whole city."

**Packages (Minneapolis market rates, verified $1,500-4,000 for builds, $150-300/hr AI consulting):**
- **Starter site** — clean one-pager, mobile, menu, hours, reservation/contact links, basic SEO. $1,500-2,000.
- **Full presence** — multi-page site + Google Business Profile cleanup + social setup + a simple booking/contact flow. $2,500-4,000.
- **AI add-on** — set up the tools that answer their FAQs, draft their posts, handle their reviews. $150-250/hr or bundled.

**Who to pitch first:** businesses already in your guide that have a weak or no
website. You already know they exist and you already feature them, so the
opening is warm: "I put you in my guide, I noticed your site could do more, here
is what I'd do."

## B. Featured-listing sell sheet  [the recurring local cash]

**What a featured listing is:** your business sits at the top of its category on
bestofmpls.com with a "Featured" mark, the styling is already built. Real people
use the guide to decide where to go; a featured spot sends them to you.

**The math that closes it:** an average Twin Cities dinner is ~$45 a head, ~$90 a
table. A $99/month listing pays for itself if it brings you ONE table a month.
Everything after that is profit. You are not buying an ad, you are buying a
couple of customers.

**Pricing:**
- **$49/month** founding rate (first 5 partners, locked 12 months, in exchange
  for a testimonial). Or
- **$99/month** standard featured listing. Or
- **Annual: $499-899** (a 15-25% discount, paid up front).

**What's included:** top placement in your category, the "Featured" badge, your
real info and a written-with-care blurb, and a spot that benefits from the site's
SEO. Clearly labeled as a paid placement, because trust is the whole product.

**Setup needed (do this before calling):**
- A Stripe payment link or simple invoice for $49 / $99 / annual.
- The `featured: true` flag is already wired in the site code; flip it on a data
  entry and the placement renders. (I can do that the moment someone pays.)

**The call script (short):**
"Hi, this is Josh with Best of MPLS, the Twin Cities guide. You're already in it.
I'm opening a small number of featured spots for local places I actually rate,
and I'd love [business] to be one. It's $49 a month for the first few partners,
top of your category, clearly marked. It pays for itself with one table. Can I
send you the details?"

**Start with Icehouse.** It's your anchor relationship. Even if they don't buy,
they'll tell you what would make them buy, and that's worth the call.
