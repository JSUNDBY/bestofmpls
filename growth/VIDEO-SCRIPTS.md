# Best of MPLS — Short-Video Scripts (YouTube Shorts + IG/TikTok Reels)

Faceless and low-production: most are a **screen recording of the site** with
text on top, so you never have to be on camera or shoot the city. Vertical 9:16,
15–30s, hook in the first 1.5 seconds. Captions in brand voice (no em dashes; keep
emojis out of the on-screen copy, fine to use one or two in the post caption).

How to make one fast: screen-record the site on your phone (or QuickTime + a phone
mirror), drop it into CapCut, add the on-screen text beats below, a trending sound,
and the end card from `brand-kit/`. Always end on the URL.

Post cadence: aim for 3–4/week. Lead with #1, #3 (timely), and #5 this week.

---

## 1. "What's happening tonight" — the core promise
- **Show:** screen-record the homepage TONIGHT block scrolling (the "X happening",
  the show pick, the where).
- **Hook (0–1.5s):** "Stop asking the group chat what's going on tonight."
- **Beats:** "Minneapolis + St. Paul, every night" → "what's actually good, with a
  time and a location" → "updated four times a day" → "free."
- **Caption:** What to do in the Twin Cities tonight, in one place. No app, no scrolling six feeds. bestofmpls.com
- **CTA:** "Save this. Link in bio."

## 2. "I put 500+ of the best spots on one map"
- **Show:** screen-record the /map/ — pan/zoom across the metro, tap a pin.
- **Hook:** "Every spot in the Twin Cities worth your time, on one map."
- **Beats:** "500+ places" → "food, bars, coffee, music, the hidden stuff" → tap a
  pin to show the detail → "filter by neighborhood."
- **Caption:** A map of the Twin Cities worth keeping. bestofmpls.com/map
- **CTA:** "Find your neighborhood. Link in bio."

## 3. Pride weekend (TIMELY — post now)
- **Show:** screen-record the /pride/ page (the essentials, the parade info).
- **Hook:** "Everything for Twin Cities Pride this weekend, in one place."
- **Beats:** "The free festival at Loring Park" → "the parade down Hennepin Sunday
  at 11" → "and where to keep it going after."
- **Caption:** Happy Pride, Twin Cities. Festival, parade, and where to celebrate. bestofmpls.com/pride
- **CTA:** "Send it to whoever you're going with."

## 4. "One calendar for every show in the metro"
- **Show:** screen-record /calendar/ scrolling the by-day stream + the venue filter.
- **Hook:** "Every show in the Twin Cities is on one calendar now."
- **Beats:** "895 events" → "every venue, First Ave to the corner bar" → "filter by
  room or by vibe" → "refreshed all day."
- **Caption:** The only Twin Cities events calendar you need. bestofmpls.com/calendar
- **CTA:** "Bookmark it. Link in bio."

## 5. "Best patios in Minneapolis" (seasonal)
- **Show:** screen-record the patios guide; or 3–4 quick b-roll clips of patios if
  you have them.
- **Hook:** "Patio season is short. Don't waste it on a bad one."
- **Beats:** name 3–4 standout patios with the neighborhood on screen → "the rest
  are in the guide."
- **Caption:** Where to actually sit outside in the Twin Cities this summer. bestofmpls.com
- **CTA:** "Which one's missing? Tell me."

## 6. "New Twin Cities spots worth knowing"
- **Show:** screen-record /new/ — the New & Notable list.
- **Hook:** "Twelve places that just opened in the Twin Cities."
- **Beats:** flash 4–5 names + one-line each (Indigenous BBQ at Šhotá, masa at Oro
  by Nixta, Dangerous Man is back...) → "full list in the guide."
- **Caption:** What just opened in MSP, worth the reservation. bestofmpls.com/new
- **CTA:** "Save for your next night out."

## 7. "Take them to..." — the situational tool
- **Show:** screen-record /take-them-to/ picking a situation (first date, out-of-towners).
- **Hook:** "Out-of-towners coming? Here's exactly where to take them."
- **Beats:** pick "out-of-towners" → show the picks → "first date, big group, snow
  day, it's all in here."
- **Caption:** The Twin Cities cheat sheet for every situation. bestofmpls.com
- **CTA:** "Steal this for your next visitor."

## 8. "Why I built a guide to my city" (the story)
- **Show:** text-on-screen over slow b-roll or quiet screen-recording of the site.
- **Hook:** "I got tired of not knowing what was happening in my own city."
- **Beats:** "So I built the guide I wanted" → "what's good tonight, a real
  calendar, 500+ spots" → "no fluff, no algorithm, just the metro" → "it's free and
  it's growing."
- **Caption:** I'm building bestofmpls.com — a living guide to Minneapolis and St. Paul. Tell me what's missing.
- **CTA:** "What would make it genuinely useful to you? Comment it."
